One site, five designs

The same facts, the same sources and the same two calls to action, in five designs. Each has its own address, so you can open them side by side, on a phone and on a larger screen.

The home page shows the First Pass. José chooses which design becomes the home page. The bar at the top of every page says which design you are looking at and steps to the next one.

  1. Design 1 of 5 · the home page

    First Pass

    The site as the AgentiCCells site factory first wrote it: a plain header, one centred column and cards, over several pages.

    Layout
    Four pages, one centred column, cards.
    Type
    The device's own sans, at one modest scale.
    Navigation
    Links in the header to each page.
    Colour
    Teal and warm orange; light or dark, following the device.
    Address
    /

  2. Design 2 of 5

    The Trace

    His work shown the way his own app shows an agent: as a trace you can read step by step. Dark, monospaced and exact, for a technical reader who wants evidence.

    Layout
    One page. A fixed rail on the left, then a run summary, numbered steps on a timeline and rules.
    Type
    Monospace for headings, labels and data; sans for paragraphs.
    Navigation
    Eight numbered sections: a list in the rail, a strip of chips on a phone.
    Colour
    Dark only: green for done, amber for figures, coral for refused.
    Address
    /d/trace/

  3. Design 3 of 5

    The Flow

    The site drawn as one of his automations: connected steps on a canvas, from the first idea to the point where a person presses send. Light and friendly, for someone with a project in mind.

    Layout
    One page. A wire runs down a dotted canvas and every section is a node on it.
    Type
    A rounded sans with heavy headings.
    Navigation
    A stepper of pills that stays at the top; it scrolls sideways on a phone.
    Colour
    Light only: teal and orange, with plum kept for the person who sends.
    Address
    /d/flow/

  4. Design 4 of 5

    The Fact Sheet

    Everything a hiring manager needs on one clean sheet, quick to scan and good on paper. White, typographic and quiet.

    Layout
    One page. A white sheet: three key figures, then numbered sections in two columns.
    Type
    A Swiss grotesque: a very large name and big numerals.
    Navigation
    A plain numbered index line; nothing sticks.
    Colour
    Black on white with one blue. Prints on two A4 pages.
    Address
    /d/sheet/

  5. Design 5 of 5

    The Arena

    Loud and playful, in the spirit of the game he built: five levels, each harder than the last. Big type and colour blocks, for meetups, workshops and anyone who should remember him.

    Layout
    One page. Full-width colour bands, with the five levels drawn as a staircase.
    Type
    Heavy capitals for headings, condensed where the device has such a face.
    Navigation
    Five links: a strip at the top, and a bar at the bottom of a phone.
    Colour
    Indigo, yellow, coral and mint, the same on every device.
    Address
    /d/arena/

What stays the same

  • Every fact and figure, each with its source and the date it was read. The master copy is the site as Markdown.
  • The two calls to action: hiring for an AI role, or an automation project.
  • Photos and videos and Use with AI, which are shared pages.
  • What is still to come: contact details, and José's own photos and videos.
