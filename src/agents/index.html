Point your agent at this site

How the site is put together

For anyone, person or agent, who is about to change the site.

Five designs, one set of facts

  • /: The Flow, the default, in src/index.html. One self-contained page with its own styles and no script.
  • /d/trace/, /d/sheet/ and /d/arena/: The Trace, The Fact Sheet and The Arena. Each is one self-contained page, src/d/NAME/index.html.
  • /d/first/: First Pass, the first version, with its pages work/, how-he-works/ and for-you/ and the stylesheet style.css.
  • /designs/ compares them, /designs.json lists them, and their small screenshots are in src/assets/designs/.
  • This page and Photos and videos are shared by all five, and use style.css.
  • Every page starts with the same skip link and design bar, styled by assets/chrome.css.

Changing a fact

  1. Get the exact words and the source from José: a link, or "José, in his own words" with the date.
  2. Change index.md first. It is the master copy of the facts.
  3. Make the same change in all five designs and in work/, how-he-works/ and for-you/. Search src/ for the old wording so that nothing is left behind.
  4. Update llms.txt if a page or a source changed.

Adding a photo or a video

  1. Use only material José has supplied and agreed to publish. Ask him what it shows, who made it, who else is in it and whether they have agreed, and for the caption in his words.
  2. Remove the file's metadata, including any location, and size it for the web. Photos: JPEG, at most 1600 pixels on the long side. Videos: MP4 with a poster image; if a video is over about 20 MB, do not commit it: link to it.
  3. Save it under src/media/. The repository is public, so a file is public from the moment it is pushed, on any branch, before any release.
  4. Put it in the media-grid block on photos-and-videos/ and in the Photos and videos section of each design, in place of the matching placeholder, with this markup (on the home page the path starts media/; in a design under d/ it starts ../../media/):
<figure class="media">
  <img src="../media/photos/NAME.jpg" alt="What is in the picture"
       width="1600" height="1067" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>The caption, in José's words.
    <span class="credit">Supplied by José, DATE</span></figcaption>
</figure>

<figure class="media">
  <video controls preload="metadata" playsinline
         poster="../media/videos/NAME.jpg" width="1280" height="720">
    <source src="../media/videos/NAME.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  </video>
  <figcaption>The caption, in José's words.
    <span class="credit">Supplied by José, DATE</span></figcaption>
</figure>

The alt text says what is in the picture. A video always has visible controls and never starts by itself. Retake the small screenshots in src/assets/designs/ when a design changes visibly: 1440 by 900 pixels for the wide one, 390 by 844 for the narrow one.

Making another design the home page

José chooses the default. To change it, swap the two files: the chosen design goes to src/index.html and the present home page to src/d/NAME/index.html. Correct the relative links in both, then update the design bar on every page, designs.json, designs/, llms.txt, index.md and the header links on the shared pages. Build and check every link before release.
