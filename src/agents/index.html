How the site is put together

For anyone, person or agent, who is about to change the site.

The pages

  • /: the home page, src/index.html. One self-contained page with its own styles, holding every section: who José is and where he works now, the tools he uses, his Web Summit work and agent team, how he works, what he can do for you, use with AI and contact, with the sources as small print in the footer. Its background answers the pointer through one small script, assets/grid.js; the page works without it.
  • The home page is written in the first person ("I build"). index.md, llms.txt and this page are in the third person, because agents read them.
  • Photos are in src/media/photos/: José's portrait and a photo of the Web Summit main stage, both on the home page and on Photos and videos.
  • This page and Photos and videos use the stylesheet style.css.
  • work/, how-he-works/ and for-you/ hold no content: they forward to the matching section of the home page, so older links keep working.
  • Every page starts with the same skip link, styled by assets/chrome.css.

Changing a fact

  1. Get the exact words and the source from José: a link, or "José, in his own words" with the date.
  2. Change index.md first. It is the master copy of the facts.
  3. Make the same change on the home page, in the first person. Search src/ for the old wording so that nothing is left behind.
  4. Update llms.txt if a page or a source changed.
  5. REALITY.md should then say what now exists. It is outside the files listed above, so get José's yes before touching it; whether editors may change it freely is for Dinis Cruz to say.

Adding a photo or a video

  1. Use only material José has supplied and agreed to publish. Ask him what it shows, who made it, who else is in it and whether they have agreed, and for the caption in his words. Blur or crop out anything that names another person, such as a name badge.
  2. Remove the file's metadata, including any location, and size it for the web. Photos: JPEG, at most 1600 pixels on the long side. Videos: MP4 with a poster image; if a video is over about 20 MB, do not commit it: link to it.
  3. Save it under src/media/. The repository is public, so a file is public from the moment it is pushed, on any branch, before any release.
  4. Put it in the media-grid block on photos-and-videos/, in place of the matching placeholder, with this markup. Put it on the home page only if José wants it there; the path there starts media/, and the two photos now on it carry a description for screen readers and no caption.
<figure class="media">
  <img src="../media/photos/NAME.jpg" alt="What is in the picture"
       width="1600" height="1067" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>The caption, in José's words.
    <span class="credit">Supplied by José, DATE</span></figcaption>
</figure>

<figure class="media">
  <video controls preload="metadata" playsinline
         poster="../media/videos/NAME.jpg" width="1280" height="720">
    <source src="../media/videos/NAME.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  </video>
  <figcaption>The caption, in José's words.
    <span class="credit">Supplied by José, DATE</span></figcaption>
</figure>

The alt text says what is in the picture. A video always has visible controls and never starts by itself.
